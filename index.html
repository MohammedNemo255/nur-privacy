01Introduction

Nur App ("نور", "we", "our", or "the App") is an Islamic companion application developed by Nemodroid. It provides prayer times, Holy Quran reading, Adhkar, Islamic knowledge quizzes, Tasbih counter, and related features.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the App, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Nur App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

03How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and improve core app features (prayer times, Quran reader, quiz battles).
  • Identify your account across sessions and devices.
  • Display your quiz points, level, and battle history inside the App.
  • Send local notifications for prayer times and reminders — we do not send marketing messages.
  • Diagnose crashes and improve stability (anonymous, aggregated only).

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond what is needed to operate the App.

05Data Storage & Security

Your account profile and quiz progress are stored in Google Cloud Firestore with industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. App preferences (Azan settings, language) are stored locally on your device using Android DataStore and are never transmitted.

Location coordinates are used momentarily to compute prayer times and are not transmitted to our servers or stored anywhere outside your device.

We implement reasonable technical safeguards; however, no system is completely secure. If you believe your data has been compromised, please contact us immediately.

06Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We share data only in the following limited cases:

  • Service providers: Firebase and Huawei as described in Section 4, solely to operate the App.
  • Legal requirements: If required by applicable law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of users or the public.

07Your Rights

Depending on your country of residence, you may have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — update inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion — request that we delete your account and associated data.
  • Withdrawal of consent — revoke location permission at any time in your device Settings.
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 10. We will respond within 30 days.

To delete your account data: log out of the App, then contact us at the email below with your display name or account email. We will permanently remove your Firestore records within 7 business days.

08Children's Privacy

Nur App is rated for users aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

09Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

05数据存储与安全

您的账户资料和问答进度存储在 Google Cloud Firestore 中，采用行业标准的传输加密（TLS）和静态加密。应用偏好（召唤礼拜设置、语言）使用 Android DataStore 存储在您的设备本地，不会被传输。

位置坐标仅用于即时计算礼拜时间，不会传输到我们的服务器，也不会存储在您设备以外的任何地方。

我们采取合理的技术保障措施；但没有任何系统是完全安全的。如果您认为您的数据已被泄露，请立即联系我们。
